Model limits & safety context

This is an educational animation, not a biological simulator, treatment recommendation, or efficacy comparison. Motion, contact duration, risk categories, and particle counts are illustrative. Both T and NK cells can disengage and kill serially; their behavior is not a strict slow-versus-fast dichotomy.

CAR-NK approaches for neuroblastoma are investigational. Donor-derived does not mean a product is immediately available. Manufacturing, access, CRS, and other risks vary by product and patient; CRS can occur with either approach.

Autologous CAR-T generally does not cause conventional allogeneic GvHD. “None modeled” for CAR-NK refers to this simplified NK-intrinsic model, not zero clinical risk or a guarantee about a donor product.

Reduced MHC-I can contribute to NK “missing-self” recognition, but NK activity also depends on activating and inhibitory signals. That balance, tumor resistance, normal-tissue GD2, trafficking, and clinical outcomes are not simulated. Green beads symbolize perforin/granzyme delivery, not measured molecules; surface flashes do not establish tumor death.
